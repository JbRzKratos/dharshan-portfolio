# Magazine archive notes

The local website includes 28 magazine/layout projects and one video edition. Every supplied finished page export is included. Two-page Photoshop print spreads were rendered with psd-tools and separated into individual pages. The political editorial contains the eight supplied interior design pages; a final cover was not present.

Input-only folders and reference photographs were excluded from the publication reader. Duplicate source folders were matched to their final exports. Administrative material (price quotation), raw content documents, general studio graphics and mosaic source files are outside the magazine collection. The Manju Birthday and Rockstar shortcuts point to deleted originals; the separate Rockstar Birthday PNG export is included.

The Sri Mathy folder contained a portfolio video rather than individual pages. The complete video is available on demand with preload=none. Its original file is linked in the film reader.

Names were cleaned up for display, retaining sourceTitle in the data. Descriptions summarize the supplied visuals and edition subjects; they do not invent project briefs, results, dates or individual credits. This is a local site, not a public deployment.
